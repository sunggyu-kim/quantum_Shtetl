# Scott Aaronson Idea Atlas - Topic Taxonomy Draft

## Top-level domains
1. **Quantum Computing**
   - quantum advantage / supremacy
   - fault tolerance
   - algorithms
   - hardware skepticism
   - post-quantum / crypto implications

2. **Complexity Theory**
   - P vs NP
   - circuit complexity
   - sampling complexity
   - proof complexity
   - computational limits

3. **Physics Foundations**
   - quantum mechanics foundations
   - Bell / superdeterminism
   - many worlds / simulation
   - cosmology / reality / time

4. **AI & Intelligence**
   - LLMs
   - AGI / superintelligence
   - AI risk
   - human specialness
   - cognition / consciousness

5. **Civilization & Existential Risk**
   - fate of humanity
   - biosecurity / pandemics
   - nuclear / geopolitical risk
   - longtermism
   - societal collapse / resilience

6. **Science Policy & Institutions**
   - research funding
   - academia
   - publication culture
   - education
   - institutional incentives

7. **Politics & Society**
   - US politics
   - Israel / Jewish identity
   - free speech
   - culture war
   - governance / democracy

8. **Philosophy & Metaphysics**
   - meaning / morality
   - free will
   - reductionism
   - personal identity
   - epistemology

9. **Personal / Community / Meta**
   - travel / talks / interviews
   - memorials
   - personal reflections
   - community disputes
   - blog meta / AMA / announcements

## Cross-cutting dimensions
- **Tone**: explanatory / argumentative / alarmed / reflective / celebratory / elegiac / skeptical
- **Stance strength**: weak / moderate / strong
- **Temporal mode**: reactive-to-news / evergreen / retrospective / forward-looking
- **Evidence mode**: technical / empirical / philosophical / anecdotal / political

## Normalization rules
- Each post gets 1-3 primary topics and up to 5 secondary topics.
- `Announcements` alone is not a sufficient semantic topic; pair it with content topic if available.
- `The Fate of Humanity` should usually be decomposed into AI risk, bio-risk, politics, civilization, or ethics where possible.
- Personal memorial or event posts can still carry strong technical/philosophical tags if the body supports it.
