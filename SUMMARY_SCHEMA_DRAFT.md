# Summary Schema Draft

## MonthSummary
- `month`: YYYYMM
- `headline_themes`: 3-7 items
- `dominant_topics`: ranked list
- `new_or_shifting_ideas`: list
- `recurring_concerns`: list
- `representative_posts`: 3-5 post ids
- `stance_changes`: list of changed or intensified positions
- `tone_profile`: ranked tones
- `external_context`: major outside events connected to that month
- `summary_narrative`: 1-3 paragraphs

## YearSummary
- `year`: YYYY
- `headline_themes`
- `topic_distribution`
- `idea_evolution`
- `major_stance_arcs`
- `important_entities`
- `best_entry_points`: recommended posts for newcomers
- `summary_narrative`: 3-6 paragraphs

## Website-ready derived views
- timeline cards
- topic graph nodes/edges
- stance timeline series
- quote evidence cards
- entity co-occurrence network
